Chocolate Fudge Cake

Ingredients:

  • 1 can of condensed milk
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup of chocolate powder
  • 4 level spoonful of butter

Instructions:

Mix all of the ingredients in a blend. Grease a pudding mold with butter and chocolate powder. Bake in water bath for about 40 minutes. When finished, place in the refrigerator until chilled. Unmold it and sprinkle chocolate sprinkles over the top.

Chocolate Cake

Ingredients:

  • 3 eggs
  • 2 cups of sugar
  • 2 cups of flour
  • 1 cup of oil
  • 1 cup of chocolate powder
  • 1 tablespoon of baking powder
  • 1 cup of boiling water

Instructions:

In a great bowl, add the ingredients one at a time, in the order listed, and mix well after each addition. Take to bake in a greased mold for about 30 minutes.
