Estrogonofe

Ingredients:

  • 1kg of diced chicken breast
  • Finely chopped onion and garlic
  • 2 tablespoons of butter
  • 1 tablespoon of salt
  • 1 tablespoons of curry powder
  • 5 sliced tomatoes
  • 1 tablespoon of ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon of mustard
  • Milk cream

Instructions:

In a pan, melt the butter and sauté the onion and garlic until golden. Add the chicken and cook until done. Close to the end, add the salt and let it release water. Add the sliced tomatoes and cook until the sauce takes form. Add the ketchup, mustard, and curry powder. Mix well and let it cook for a few more minutes. Finally, add the milk cream and mix until it is well incorporated. Serve with rice and potato sticks.

Minute Bread

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup of all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon of yeast (cake yeast)
  • 2 tablespoons of butter
  • 1 teaspoon of sugar
  • ½ teaspoon of salt
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1 egg

Instructions:

In a bowl, mix the flour, yeast, sugar, and salt. Add the milk, egg, and butter. Mix until a smooth dough forms and divide the dough into small portions and shape them into rolls. Let it rest for 40 to 50 minutes. Bake for 20-25 minutes or until golden brown.
