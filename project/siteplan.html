Site Plan

Site Name

Name: Domingues Family Recipes

I chose this name because the site will be dedicated to some recipes from my mother's side of the family.

Site Purpose

The purpose is to preserve and share a few family recipes, keeping my mother's culinary tradition alive and allowing other to try them as well.

Scenarios

  • What is the fastest way to bake bread?
  • What do I need to do to ensure the bread dough turns out really fluffy?

Color Schema

Main color: Brown (#8B4513) – to convey coziness and tradition.

Secundary color: Cream (#FFFDD0) – to add lightness and contrast.

Typography

Main font: Roboto – used for paragraphs to ensure easy reading.

Secondary font: Playfair Display – used for headings to give a classic and elegant touch.
